About Mildfire

Fire, understood and tamed — not just extinguished.

Mildfire Safety & Protection Technologies Ltd is built on a simple premise: fire is not something you merely react to, it's something you engineer against in advance. Our name reflects that philosophy — taking the destructive, uncontrolled threat of fire and transforming it, through technology, into something mild: understood, monitored and neutralized before it becomes a crisis.

We don't think of ourselves as an equipment vendor. Equipment is a component. What we actually deliver is peace of mind — for facility managers, business owners and homeowners who need to know their people and property are protected by a system that was actually designed for their specific risk, not sold off a shelf.

What we believe

Two commitments run through everything we build.

Customer-centric care

A safety system only works if it's built around how a client actually operates. We start with your risk and your constraints, not a standard package.

Sustainable growth

Systems that scale as your organization does — so protection doesn't have to be re-engineered from scratch every time your footprint changes.

Who we serve

Three environments, one standard of care.

  1. Commercial clients

    Offices, retail and hospitality spaces where occupant safety and business continuity both matter.

  2. Industrial clients

    Plants, process facilities and storage sites with elevated hazard profiles and strict compliance requirements.

  3. Residential clients

    Homes and residential developments that need reliable, monitored protection without added complexity.
